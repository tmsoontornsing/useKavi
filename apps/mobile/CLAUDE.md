## Mascot

Kavi is a mason jar with the K on its label. Use `<Mascot pose="wave" />` from `src/components/Mascot.tsx`; poses are wave, think, cook, shop, cheer, oops, sleep and peek (see `MASCOT_INFO` for where each belongs). Draw and edit poses in `scripts/build-mascot.mjs`, then run `node scripts/build-mascot.mjs` to regenerate `assets/mascot/*.svg` and `src/components/mascotPoses.generated.ts`. Do not edit the generated files by hand.
