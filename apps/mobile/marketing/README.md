# Kavi marketing

Everything here is generated by `scripts/build-brand.mjs`. Edit the script, then run
`node scripts/build-mascot.mjs && node scripts/build-brand.mjs` from `apps/mobile`.

| File | Size | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `logo.svg`, `logo.png` | 600 x 280 | Pip beside the wordmark, on light backgrounds |
| `logo-dark.svg`, `logo-dark.png` | 600 x 280 | The same on dark backgrounds |
| `pip-mark.svg` | 200 x 200 | Pip alone, for avatars and small spaces |
| `poster-1-plan.png` to `poster-5-cook.png` | 1290 x 2796 | Ads and the lead App Store image. Apple wants most store screenshots to show the app, so put real screen captures after the first poster. |
| `social-card.png` | 1200 x 630 | Link previews (Open Graph, X, iMessage) |
| `play-feature.png` | 1024 x 500 | Google Play feature graphic |

## Store listing

**Name:** Kavi: Meal Plans on a Budget

**Subtitle (30 characters max):** Weekly meals inside your budget

**Promotional text:**
Meet Pip, the jar that plans your week. Tell Kavi who you cook for and what you spend, and Pip fills the week with meals that fit, plus one grocery list to shop from.

**Description:**
Kavi plans every meal of your week inside your grocery budget.

Tell Pip, Kavi's jar, who you cook for, what you spend, and what everyone eats. Pip plans breakfast, lunch, and dinner for the week and shows what it costs before you buy a thing.

- Plans around your budget, diet, and allergies.
- Puts the foods you love first. Like curry and pasta? They show up first.
- Builds one grocery list for the whole week. What you already have in your pantry stays off it.
- Swap any meal you are not feeling for one that still fits the week.

Free includes one plan a month. Kavi Plus plans every week.

**Keywords (100 characters max):**
meal planner,budget,grocery list,meal prep,family meals,recipes,cheap meals,weekly plan,pantry
