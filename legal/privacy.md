# Kavi Privacy Policy

> Kavi collects what it needs to plan your meals: your account, household size, budget, diet, allergies, food preferences, pantry, and lists. We do not sell your information, show ads, or track you across other apps. You can delete your account and everything in it from Settings.

Effective [EFFECTIVE DATE]

This Privacy Policy explains what information [COMPANY LEGAL NAME] ("we", "us") collects through the Kavi app and related services ("Kavi"), how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. It applies to everyone who uses Kavi.

## 1. Information you give us

- Account. Your email address, and your password if you sign up with email (we store only a secure hash of it). If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your email address (Apple may give us a private relay address instead) and a user ID, and Google may also share your name and profile picture.
- Household profile. How many people you cook for, your weekly grocery budget, your diet (for example vegetarian or halal), allergies, foods you dislike, foods you like, and the kitchen equipment you have.
- Your lists. Pantry items, meal plans, meals you swap, grocery lists and what you check off, and steps you mark done.
- Household members. The people you invite to share your grocery list, and their accounts.
- Preferences. Settings such as reminder and notification choices.
- Support. What you send us when you contact us.

## 2. Information collected automatically

Our hosting and sign-in providers record technical information needed to run and secure Kavi, such as your IP address, device and app version, and the times you sign in or make requests. Kavi does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking software from other companies, does not use an advertising identifier, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

## 3. Sensitive information

Your allergies, dislikes, and diet can reveal information about your health or, in the case of a choice such as halal, your religious beliefs. Some laws treat this as sensitive or "consumer health" data. We collect it only because you choose to give it, and use it only to plan your meals and grocery lists. You can leave it blank, change it, or delete it in Settings at any time. We never sell it, use it for advertising, or use it to make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

## 4. How we use information

- To provide Kavi: plan meals that fit your household, budget, diet, allergies, and kitchen; estimate prices and nutrition; build and sync your grocery list; and share it with the people you invite.
- To manage your account and Kavi Plus subscription status.
- To send notifications you turn on, and service messages such as changes to these policies.
- To respond to you, fix problems, and keep Kavi secure, including preventing fraud and abuse.
- To improve Kavi using information that does not identify you.
- To comply with the law and enforce our Terms.

We do not sell your personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use it to show you ads.

## 5. Who we share it with

- Service providers that run Kavi for us, under contracts that limit how they may use it: Supabase (database, sign-in, and hosting, in the United States); and Apple and Google (sign-in, and processing subscription payments and refunds).
- AI meal planning. To create a plan, we may send an AI service provider (currently Anthropic) the details needed to choose meals: household size, budget, diet, allergies, dislikes, foods you like, and the list of candidate recipes. We do not send your name, email address, or account ID.
- If we add a subscription management provider (such as RevenueCat), it will receive an anonymous app user ID and your purchase records from Apple or Google.
- People in your household see your shared grocery list.
- For legal reasons: when required by law or valid legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and property of you, others, or us.
- In a business transfer, such as a merger or sale of assets, subject to this Policy.

## 6. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account exists. When you delete your account in Settings, we delete your account, household profile, pantry, meal plans, grocery lists, and household links from our active systems right away; copies in backups are overwritten within 30 days. We may keep limited information longer when the law requires it or to resolve disputes. Apple and Google keep their own purchase records.

## 7. Your choices and rights

- Change your details any time in Settings, and turn notifications on or off there or in your device settings.
- Delete your account and its data: Settings, Delete Account.
- Ask for a copy of your information, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing [CONTACT EMAIL].

Depending on where you live, for example California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Washington, and other US states, or the EU and UK, you may have the right to know what personal information we hold and how we use it, to access and receive a portable copy, to correct it, to delete it, to limit the use of sensitive information, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of sale, sharing for advertising, and profiling. Because we do not sell or share personal information for advertising, there is nothing to opt out of; if that ever changes, we will honor Global Privacy Control signals. We will not discriminate against you for using your rights.

To make a request, email [CONTACT EMAIL] from the address on your account. We will verify your request and respond within the time the law requires, usually 45 days. You may use an authorized agent where the law allows. If we deny your request, you can appeal by replying to our decision, and we will respond to your appeal within the time the law requires. If you are not satisfied, you can contact your state attorney general or, in the EU or UK, your data protection authority.

## 8. Consumer health data (Washington, Nevada, and similar laws)

The allergies, dislikes, and diet you enter may be consumer health data under laws such as Washington's My Health My Data Act. We collect it from you, use it only to provide the meal planning you ask for, share it only with the service providers listed in section 5 and the household members you invite, and never sell it. You can see, change, or delete it in Settings, or ask us to delete it and to tell you which third parties have received it, using the contact details below.

## 9. If you are in the EU, UK, or Switzerland

We process your information to perform our contract with you (to provide Kavi), for our legitimate interests (keeping Kavi secure and improving it), to comply with legal obligations, and with your consent (notifications, and the allergy and diet details you choose to enter, which you can withdraw by removing them). Your information is stored and processed in the United States. Where the law requires, we protect transfers with safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 10. Children

Kavi is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact [CONTACT EMAIL] and we will delete it.

## 11. Security

We protect your information with measures such as encryption in transit, access controls that limit each account to its own data, and keeping secret keys off your device. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee security. If a breach affects your information, we will notify you as the law requires.

## 12. Changes to this Policy

We will update this Policy when our practices change. If a change is material, we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect, and ask for your consent where the law requires it.
